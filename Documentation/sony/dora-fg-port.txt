Sony Dora legacy QPNP fuel gauge on Linux 4.19
============================================

Source and scope
----------------

The qpnp-fg.c and Sony extension sources come from the working Tone 4.4
reference, sbot110/android_kernel_sony_msm8996 commit
0c295b15b6d0fb59a64e13b9e0ef2070818ef2a1, under
drivers/power/supply/qcom/. They implement the original PMIC SRAM fuel gauge,
battery profiles, Sony capacity learning, aging and JEITA controls. This is
not a replacement with the FG GEN3/GEN4 register layout.

The source supports the PMI8994 and PMI8996 PMIC subtypes used by the original
Dora DT variants. Neither subtype is assigned to F8131 or F8132 by model name.
The original board/revision DT selection remains necessary for both models.

Changes for 4.19
---------------

* Register accesses use the parent SPMI regmap, with original hardware register
  offsets, interrupt names, SRAM tables and Sony battery algorithms retained.
* Native wakeup_source objects replace the removed Android wake-lock API.
  Allocation errors propagate; devres unregisters successfully allocated
  sources. IRQ registration/wake ownership is tracked for unwind/removal.
* power_supply_config is initialized, registration is checked, and teardown
  explicitly unregisters the BMS after asynchronous users are stopped.
* Legacy integer properties are appended to power_supply_property and to the
  matching sysfs array. Existing property IDs and the bounded string-property
  range are retained. FV_CFG retains Sony's vfloat_mv sysfs name.
* Cached USB/DC/battery supply references are acquired under a per-device
  mutex and released by devres. Temporary BMS lookups in module parameter
  setters are paired with power_supply_put(); setters are serialized against
  teardown and reject a stopping provider.
* Hard-JEITA alarmtimer callbacks queue sleepable work. The timer callback
  performs no power_supply or regmap operations. A dedicated wakeup_source
  covers the worker, polling remains ten seconds, and teardown cancels both
  the timer and worker.
* All initialized works, including battery_age_work, are drained. The init
  worker does not synchronously cancel itself. Alarm rearming is checked
  against the stopping flag.
* Sony sysfs and debugfs interfaces are removed before BMS teardown. Initial
  hardware work starts after Sony sysfs registration, so an initialization
  failure cannot race with creation of new attributes. Partial debugfs setup
  clears its stored pointers, and file operations pin the module.
* PMIC-revision phandles and early-exit child-node references are put.
  Deferred IRQ/provider errors are preserved. Unknown FG peripheral subtypes
  fail probe, and an unsuccessful SOC interrupt-status read cannot be logged
  as an initialized value.
* Time accounting uses a real configurable RTC via QPNP_FG_RTC_DEVICE; no
  synthetic RTC or voltage/current readings were introduced.

Configuration and DT dependencies
---------------------------------

For built-in Dora integration, enable:

  CONFIG_QPNP_FG=y
  CONFIG_QPNP_FG_EXTENSION=y
  CONFIG_QPNP_FG_RTC_DEVICE="rtc0"
  CONFIG_RTC_CLASS=y
  CONFIG_RTC_DRV_PM8XXX=y

QPNP_FG also requires MFD_SPMI_PMIC and PM_SLEEP, and selects OF_BATTERYDATA
and QPNP_REVID. RTC_HCTOSYS is optional: the private RTC device setting uses
RTC_HCTOSYS_DEVICE when available, otherwise defaults to rtc0. The native
PM8994 DT uses qcom,pm8941-rtc, which RTC_DRV_PM8XXX actually matches.

The driver matches qcom,qpnp-fg. A functioning board DT must supply the
original FG SOC/battery/memory-interface children and named IRQs, PMIC-revid
phandle, Dora battery-data profile and Tone/Dora temperature and termination
settings. The original locations are:

  arch/arm/boot/dts/qcom/msm-pmi8994.dtsi
  arch/arm/boot/dts/qcom/msm8996-tone-common.dtsi
  arch/arm/boot/dts/qcom/msm8996-tone-dora-common.dtsi
  arch/arm/boot/dts/qcom/batterydata-tone-dora-send.dtsi

For example, the Dora override selects its own battery-data node, 150 mA
termination settings and 20 deci-C JEITA hysteresis. These must not be
replaced by another Tone model's profile to obtain a successful probe.

The dora power DT supplement now supplies the original legacy FG children,
revision phandle and Dora profile for PM8994/PMI8994. Its 32 power/profile
nodes were compared against the flattened Sony reference; see dora-power-dt.txt.
This source/DT activation is not proof of successful probe or accuracy.
A charger exposing the original battery power_supply controls is also required: battery charging
enable, JEITA thresholds and Sony FV_CFG/FV_CMP_CFG aging controls are real
inter-driver dependencies. VADC/BCL and charger integration are separate
ports; the FG reads its own SRAM/regmap directly rather than a fake ADC shim.

Validation and physical testing
-------------------------------

The initial isolated configuration linked a complete ARM64 vmlinux with
QPNP_FG and the Sony extension enabled. The final reviewed source was checked
again by building drivers/power/supply/qcom/qpnp-fg.o with ARM64 GCC 12.2 and
the 4.19 tree's normal strict API checks. The latest combined build and validated source revision are recorded in
dora-build-validation.txt; object compilation alone does not cover hardware.

No phone test has been performed for this 4.19 FG port. F8131 and F8132 each
need separate checks of PMIC revision selection, measured voltage/current/
temperature and battery ID, profile selection, SOC across reboot/suspend,
USB insertion/removal, normal and warm/cool charging, hot/cold charge cutoff,
termination, aging controls and shutdown. Battery safety behavior cannot be
established by compilation or by the display evidence from the working 4.4
F8132 kernel.
