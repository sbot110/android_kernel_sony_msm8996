Dora MSM8996: UFS GCC/CX voltage-corner blocker
===========================================

Reference: Sony 4.4 commit 0c295b15b6d0fb59a64e13b9e0ef2070818ef2a1.
This is a source/flattened-DT audit, not a runtime voltage measurement.

P0: restored UFS bus topology does not complete safe UFS clock/power support.
The native GCC implementation can program the original UFS frequencies but
does not make the Sony digital voltage-corner votes required at those rates.
The Dora UFS host must remain disabled until that dependency is implemented.
Successful NoC registration or a host probe would not prove safe operation.

Original Sony contract
----------------------

drivers/clk/msm/clock-gcc-8996.c defines vdd_dig with
DEFINE_VDD_REGULATORS. msm_gcc_8996_probe() obtains the mandatory vdd_dig regulator;
an unavailable provider returns its error, including -EPROBE_DEFER.
The original clock framework attaches these FMAX maps to the source clocks:

 source                 LOWER      LOW         NOMINAL     HIGH
 ufs_axi_clk_src         19.2 MHz   100 MHz     200 MHz     240 MHz
 ufs_ice_core_clk_src    19.2 MHz   150 MHz     300 MHz     --

These are voltage thresholds, not an additional list of programmable rates.
The actual source frequency tables are 100/200/240 MHz for AXI and
19.2/150/300 MHz for ICE. No UFS rate increase beyond Sony is needed.

drivers/clk/msm/vdd-level-8996.h maps LOW to
RPM_REGULATOR_CORNER_SVS_SOC (logical 4), NOMINAL to CORNER_NORMAL (5), and
HIGH to CORNER_SUPER_TURBO (7). GCC probe remaps LOWER to SVS_KRAIT (3).
These regulator-API integers are corners, not microvolts. The original RPM
regulator subtracts CORNER_NONE (1) before sending the "corn" wire value.
Thus AXI 200 MHz / ICE 300 MHz require logical corner 5, wire corner 4;
the 100 MHz / 150 MHz pair still requires logical corner 4, wire corner 3.

All four final Sony Dora DTBs (v3.0/v3.1, PMI8994/PMI8996) resolve GCC's
vdd_dig-supply to /soc/qcom,rpm-smd/rpm-regulator-smpa1/regulator-s1-corner:
pm8994_s1_corner, qcom,set=3 (ACTIVE+SLEEP), min=1, max=7,
qcom,use-voltage-corner. All four final UFS nodes have the same nonzero
freq-table-hz pairs: core_clk_src=100000000/200000000 and
core_clk_unipro_src=150000000/300000000. Their other nine pairs are zero.

GCC_UFS_AXI_CLK, GCC_SYS_NOC_UFS_AXI_CLK and GCC_AGGRE2_UFS_AXI_CLK inherit
ufs_axi_clk_src. GCC_UFS_ICE_CORE_CLK inherits the ICE source;
GCC_UFS_UNIPRO_CORE_CLK uses its divide-by-two output (150 MHz at source
300 MHz). The voltage vote belongs to the source and follows its consumers.

Neither the final Sony UFS host nor the native QMP14nm PHY DT has a UFS AUX
clock. ref_aux_clk in ufs_qcom_phy_init_clks() is an optional facility for
other PHY versions. Dora PHY references are LN_BB_CLK and GCC_UFS_CLKREF_CLK;
PCIe AUX clocks must not be substituted for an absent UFS clock.

Native 4.19 gap and required work
--------------------------------

drivers/clk/qcom/gcc-msm8996.c keeps the correct frequency tables in
ftbl_ufs_axi_clk_src and ftbl_ufs_ice_core_clk_src. Its clk_rcg2 source
definitions use clk_rcg2_ops without a regulator, FMAX map, or voltage
transition hook. gcc_msm8996_probe() only maps/registers the GCC clocks and
power domains; it neither acquires vdd_dig nor implements corner voting.
Native ufshcd_init_clocks() in drivers/scsi/ufs/ufshcd.c sets the DT maximum
rates during initialization. Therefore the missing vote affects initial
bring-up, not only later frequency scaling or suspend.

Implement a coherent corner regulator frontend on the native RPM transport
and couple source-clock prepare/rate/unprepare transitions to it. Preserve
Sony corner encoding, aggregation across consumers, ACTIVE/SLEEP semantics,
ACK/error handling, voltage-before-clock increases, clock-before-voltage
decreases, and failed-transition rollback. Account for other clients of the
same CX RPM resource; separate raw-voltage and corner requests cannot race
or overwrite one another. Verify gates, initialization, scaling and resume
with failure injection before enabling the host; then measure on a device.

The native qcom_smd-regulator UV key does not implement a corner frontend.
An analog PHY supply, UFS_GDSC, a bandwidth vote, a fixed voltage/corner, or
an assumed bootloader vote does not replace this clock-dependent contract.
No experimental voltage, fabricated provider, or fixed vote was introduced.
