Dora MSM8996: eMMC and GCC/MMCC voltage corners (stage4)
======================================================

Hardware source: final Sony4.4 dora DTs and clock sources at
0c295b15b6d0fb59a64e13b9e0ef2070818ef2a1, after all v3 overrides.
Dora internal storage is SDHC1 eMMC, NOT UFS. See dora-storage-audit.md.
Old UFS frequency data is retained only for generic MSM8996 regressions.

The original vdd_dig contract is now implemented by a typed native SMD RPM
frontend and source clock fmax classes. Logical NONE/LOWER/LOW/NOMINAL/HIGH
map to 1/3/4/5/7; wire corners are logical minus1. corn/vfc and BOTH/AO/floor
aggregation use real correlated ACTIVE/SLEEP ACKs. Logical corners are not
physical microvolts. Native S1/S2 microvolt clients are explicitly disabled.

Final source contracts:
 source              LOWER       LOW          NOMINAL       HIGH
 SDCC1 apps          19.2MHz      50MHz        400MHz        --
 SDCC1 ICE           19.2MHz     150MHz        300MHz        --
 SDCC2 apps          19.2MHz      50MHz        200MHz        --
 GPLL4 main          --          --           384MHz        --
 UFS AXI (unused)    19.2MHz     100MHz        200MHz       240MHz
 USB30 master        60MHz     133.333MHz     200MHz       240MHz
 MMCC MDP          171.43MHz    275MHz        330MHz       412.5MHz

SDCC1 actual frequency table still tops out at 384 MHz (DDR root), not 400 MHz.
SDCC2 original 40/80 MHz entries are restored. GPLL0 remains fixed/read-only
at its source 600 MHz and critical for uncounted CPU/CBF auxiliary consumers;
no probe retune is performed. Raw EARLY PLL numeric IDs cannot bypass the
regulated main/source outputs on coupled Dora.

All 56 GCC / 49 MMCC maps and final override line provenance are in
 dora-clock-voltage.json
 dora-clock-voltage.md
Check-clock-voltage.py --reference-commit independently reparses those Sony
Git sources, executes the production adapter and readonly PLL callbacks,
and rejects changed map data. CCF and RPM tests exercise their actual code
separately, including ACK loss, rollback, bootstrap and lifetime failures.

GCC/MMCC supplies reference the typed CX child; MMPLL4/9 additionally use
physical L12 at exactly 1.8 V. Unknown boot-active parent ownership retains a
real acknowledged HIGH lease. Native physical regulator errors cannot be
recovered by cached UV/SWEN success: L12 and MMC uncertainty remain latched.
Physical replay/SLEEP, integrated handoff and hardware validation remain P0.
Typed CX is active for GCC/MMCC. MX, MMC and display/GPU activation gates
are retained in all four DTBs.
No fixed fake regulator or bootloader-voltage assumption replaces this work.
